Lower-fee flow

How this replaces a percentage-based ordering app

1

Customer orders direct

The customer uses this Kochi-branded menu instead of a marketplace/order app that takes a sales percentage.

2

Restaurant gets notified

Order can route to email, SMS, kitchen tablet, Google Sheet, or POS middleware. This preview includes the API boundary.

3

Payment with lower fees

Recommended launch path: Stripe Checkout for card payments, or pay-at-pickup first. Stripe is usually processor-fee only, not commission on every sale.

Owner pitch

Keep the order relationship — and more of the margin.

Menu11-like systems can be useful, but if they take a percentage of each sale, the cost compounds. A direct ordering site has upfront setup + small hosting/payment processing costs, while customer data, menu control, and brand experience stay with Kochi Po-cha.
