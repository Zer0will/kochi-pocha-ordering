Improve the QR ordering system Kochi already uses

Correct current-state assumption

Kochi already has QR table ordering. Customers scan a receipt/table QR code, open the restaurant's web menu, add items to cart, place an order, then pay online or at the counter.

Actual opportunity

The pitch is not “you need QR ordering.” The pitch is: audit the current QR provider for fees, data ownership, menu-control friction, payment flow, and POS/kitchen routing — then decide whether an owner-controlled version would be cheaper or more flexible.

Option A — keep current provider, improve around it

If the existing QR site works well and fees are reasonable, keep it. Improve photos, menu structure, Google/AI discoverability, upsell bundles, and customer-retention capture outside the ordering provider.

Option B — owner-controlled QR ordering

Rebuild the same customer behavior — scan, menu, cart, order, pay online or counter — with Kochi-controlled menu data, Stripe/pay-at-counter options, and order routing to email/SMS/tablet/POS middleware.

Phase 1 preview boundary

This demo validates the menu/cart/API flow only. It does not send real orders to Kochi, does not charge cards, and does not replace the current QR provider without owner approval and credentials.

Better pitch line

“Your current QR ordering already works. We can help you keep that convenience while checking whether the fees, data ownership, menu control, and payment flow can be improved.”
